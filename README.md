# DataCo Supply Chain Analytics & Predictive Modelling

## Overview
Capstone project analysing 180,000+ orders across a multi-table e-commerce supply chain dataset. Customer, product, order, and geography data were cleaned and integrated into a unified dataset for analysis.

## Objective
Identify the key drivers of late delivery, order cancellation, and payment risk, and quantify the financial impact of high-risk product categories and shipping modes.

## Approach
- Consolidated multi-table source data into a single analysis-ready dataset
- Performed exploratory analysis to surface late-delivery, cancellation, and payment-risk patterns
- Built a **Random Forest classification model**, trained on ~32,800 orders, to predict late-delivery risk
- Used feature importance from the model to translate results into operational recommendations

## Key Findings
- Certain product categories and shipping modes carry disproportionately higher risk and financial exposure
- Feature importance analysis highlighted the operational factors most predictive of late delivery

## Deliverables
Findings and strategic recommendations were presented in a **27-slide executive report**, covering:
- Late-delivery analysis
- Product/market trends
- Mitigation strategies
